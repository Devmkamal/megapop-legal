Delete your Mega Pop account

This page explains how to delete your Mega Pop account and the data attached to it. Mega Pop is a bubble shooter game published by NourByte.

You only have an account if you chose to sign in with Google. If you play offline, no account exists and no personal data ever leaves your device — there is nothing to delete.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, inside the game

  1. Open Mega Pop.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation dialog, then tap DELETE.

Deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email support@nourbyte.com from the email address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We verify that the request comes from the account holder before acting on it, and we complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently erases all of the following from our servers:

  • Your profile — name, email address, avatar URL and user ID
  • Your scores, level stars, campaign progress and endless-mode bests
  • Your helper inventory
  • Your purchase records
  • Your event progress
  • Your friends list and friend code
  • Your device tokens for push notifications

What is kept, and for how long

  • Security audit entries are kept for 12 months, but they are anonymized at the moment you delete your account — the link to you is removed and only the bare security event remains. We keep these to detect fraud and abuse, which we cannot do if the record disappears entirely.
  • Purchase and refund records held by Google Play or Apple are kept by those stores under their own policies, not ours. We delete our copy; deleting the store's copy is not something we can do for you. Contact the store directly.
  • Crash and analytics data is pseudonymous — it is never linked to your name or email — and expires on Firebase's default retention schedule.

Deleting your account does not refund past purchases, and helper items you bought are lost with the account.
