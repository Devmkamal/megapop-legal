Privacy Policy — Mega Pop

Effective date: 22 August 2026 Provider: NourByte, Egypt Contact: support@nourbyte.com

1. Summary

Mega Pop ("the game", "we") is a bubble-shooter game. You can play fully offline without an account. If you sign in, we sync your progress and enable leaderboards, events, and (optional) purchases. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

3. How we use data

  • Provide and sync gameplay, leaderboards, events, and purchases.
  • Keep the service secure (rate limiting, fraud/abuse prevention, audit logs).
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the game.
  • Send notifications you have opted into.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Third-party processors

  • Google / Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics) — see Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing and receipt validation.
  • Cloud hosting provider — hosts our backend and database.

5. Data retention

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Security audit logs are retained for 12 months in anonymized form.
  • Crash/analytics data follows Firebase's default retention (Firebase's default retention periods).

6. Your rights & choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time: in-app via Settings/Profile → Delete account, which permanently erases your account, scores, inventory, purchases, friendships, and device tokens (security audit entries are retained but anonymized). You may also email support@nourbyte.com.
  • Play offline: skip sign-in and no personal data leaves your device.
  • Access / correction / portability: email support@nourbyte.com.
  • Depending on your region (EEA/UK — GDPR, California — CPRA, etc.) you may have additional rights.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Access tokens are stored encrypted on device (Android Keystore). Passwords are never used (Google Sign-In only).

We may update this policy; material changes will be notified in-app or on our store listing. The "Effective date" reflects the latest version.
