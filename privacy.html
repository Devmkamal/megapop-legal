Privacy Policy — Mega Pop

Effective date: 10 September 2026 Provider: NourByte, Egypt Contact: support@nourbyte.com

1. Summary

Mega Pop ("the game", "we") is a bubble-shooter game. You can play fully offline without an account. If you sign in, we sync your progress and enable leaderboards, events, and (optional) purchases. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data, and we do not collect your device's advertising ID.

3. How we use data

  • Provide and sync gameplay, leaderboards, events, and purchases.
  • Keep the service secure (rate limiting, fraud/abuse prevention, audit logs).
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the game.
  • Send notifications you have opted into.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Third-party processors

  • Google / Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics) — see Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing and receipt validation.
  • Cloud hosting provider — hosts our backend and database.

5. Data retention

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Security audit logs are retained for 12 months in anonymized form.
  • Crash/analytics data follows Firebase's default retention (Firebase's default retention periods).

6. Your rights & choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time: in-app via Profile → Delete account, which permanently erases your account, scores, inventory, purchases, friendships, and device tokens (security audit entries are retained but anonymized). You may also email support@nourbyte.com. Step-by-step instructions: Delete your account.
  • Play offline: skip sign-in and no account data leaves your device (crash reports, and analytics for players 13 and over, still apply — see §2).
  • Access / correction / portability: email support@nourbyte.com.
  • Depending on your region (EEA/UK — GDPR, California — CPRA, etc.) you may have additional rights.

7. Children's privacy

Mega Pop is a game for all ages, including children. The first time the app opens, it asks for the player's birth year. It does not ask for a full date of birth, and the answer is kept only on the device — it is never sent to us.

If the birth year shows the player is under 13 (and before the question has been answered), the app:

  • does not offer sign-in, so we collect no name, email address, avatar, account ID or purchase history, and no push notification token is sent to our servers;
  • turns off Firebase Analytics completely, including the events it would otherwise record automatically;
  • does not offer in-app purchases and shows no purchase prompts during play;
  • keeps all game progress on the device only.

For every player, of any age, the app never collects the advertising ID and shows no ads.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics: device model, OS version, app version and the crash trace, not linked to a name or account) are still collected for all players so that we can fix crashes.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has given us personal information — for example by signing in on an earlier version of the app — email support@nourbyte.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Access tokens are stored encrypted on device (Android Keystore). Passwords are never used (Google Sign-In only).

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be notified in-app or on our store listing. The "Effective date" reflects the latest version.
