Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the game: Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, scores, inventory and purchase records from our servers and cannot be undone. If you cannot access the game, email support@nourbyte.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete it for you.

Mega Pop is made by NourByte. Questions about your data or this page: support@nourbyte.com.
